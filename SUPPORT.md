# Support

## Where to ask

**[Discord](https://discord.gg/qNyybSSPm5)** is the fastest place for a question —
setup help, "is this supposed to happen", configuration, or anything you would
rather work through in a conversation. Support is available in Russian and English.

**[Documentation](https://bxteam.org/docs)** covers the projects that have it:
[DivineMC](https://bxteam.org/docs/divinemc) and
[NDailyRewards](https://bxteam.org/docs/ndailyrewards).

**GitHub issues** are for bugs and feature requests in a specific project — things
with a reproducible case or a concrete proposal. A question is usually answered
faster on Discord.

## Filing a good bug report

The issue form asks for these, and a report without them usually turns into a
round-trip that costs you a day:

- the project **version**, and the platform or server software it runs on;
- what you expected and what happened instead;
- the steps to reproduce it;
- the **full** log or stack trace — paste it to [pastes.dev](https://pastes.dev)
  and link it rather than truncating it into the issue.

## Security

Vulnerabilities do not go in an issue. See
[SECURITY.md](https://github.com/BX-Team/.github/blob/master/SECURITY.md).
